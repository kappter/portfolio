Ferdinand Magellan Life Timeline - Key Locations and Dates
Source: https://www.worldhistory.org/timeline/Ferdinand_Magellan/

c. 1480 - Born in Villa Real (or Sabrosa), Tras os Montes, Portugal
c. 1492 - Joins royal Portuguese court as page in Lisbon
1505 - Enlisted in Portuguese fleet to India
1510 - Assists in establishment of Portuguese Goa, India
1511 - Assists in establishment of Portuguese Malacca, Malaysia
1513 - Participates in Portuguese attack on Azemmour, Morocco
1517 - Renounces Portuguese citizenship, moves to Seville, Spain
1518 - Proposal accepted by Charles I of Spain
Aug 10, 1519 - Fleet leaves Seville, Spain
Oct 3, 1519 - Fleet leaves Canary Islands
Dec 1519 - Fleet arrives in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
Mar 21, 1520 - Fleet winters at San Julián Bay, southern Argentina
Oct-Nov 1520 - Sails through Straits of Magellan, Tierra del Fuego
Dec 18, 1520 - Leaves South America coast to cross Pacific
Jan 24, 1521 - Spots first land (San Pablo Island) in Pacific
Mar 6, 1521 - Reaches Guam, Marianas Islands
Apr 7, 1521 - Reaches Philippine Islands
Apr 27, 1521 - Killed in battle on Cebu/Mactan, Philippines
