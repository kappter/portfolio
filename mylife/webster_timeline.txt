Noah Webster Life Timeline - Key Locations and Dates
Source: https://noahwebsterhouse.org/websters-schoolhouse/timeline-of-noahs-life/

1758 - Born in West Hartford, Connecticut (October 16)
1772 - West Hartford, CT (prepared for college)
1774-1778 - New Haven, CT (Yale University student)
1778 - Glastonbury, CT (schoolmaster)
1779 - Hartford, CT (Brick School House), then West Hartford, CT (moved back home)
1780 - Litchfield, CT (studied law), then Sharon, CT (opened school)
1783 - Hartford, CT (published Blue-Backed Speller, opened law office)
1785-1789 - Philadelphia, PA (moved), then New York City (founded American Magazine 1787)
1789 - Married Rebecca Greenleaf, moved back to Hartford, CT
1793 - New York City (moved back, published American Minerva newspaper)
1798 - New Haven, CT (moved with family, served in state legislature)
1812-1822 - Amherst, Massachusetts (helped found Amherst College)
1822-1843 - New Haven, CT (moved back)
1822 - Traveled to France and England (dictionary research)
1828 - Published American Dictionary of the English Language
1828 - Visited Washington, D.C. (copyright legislation)
1843 - Died in New Haven, CT (May 28, age 84), buried at Grove Street Cemetery
