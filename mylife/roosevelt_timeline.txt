Theodore Roosevelt Life Timeline - Key Locations and Dates
Source: https://www.theodoreroosevelt.org/content.aspx?page_id=22&club_id=991271&module_id=339177

1858 - Born in New York City (Oct 27)
1876-1880 - Harvard College, Cambridge, MA
1881-1884 - New York State Assembly, Albany, NY
1883-1886 - Badlands rancher, Medora, North Dakota
1885-1919 - Sagamore Hill, Oyster Bay, Long Island, NY (home)
1889-1895 - US Civil Service Commissioner, Washington, DC
1895-1897 - Police Commissioner, New York City
1897-1898 - Assistant Secretary of Navy, Washington, DC
1898 - Rough Riders, Cuba (Spanish-American War)
1898-1900 - Governor of New York, Albany, NY
1901 - Vice President, Washington, DC
1901-1909 - President of the United States, Washington, DC
1909-1910 - African safari expedition (Kenya, Uganda, Congo, Sudan, Egypt)
1910 - European tour (Italy, Austria, France, Belgium, Netherlands, Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Germany, UK)
1913-1914 - South American expedition (Brazil, Uruguay, Argentina, Chile, Paraguay)
1914 - Trip to Spain and England
1919 - Died at Sagamore Hill, Oyster Bay, NY (Jan 6)
