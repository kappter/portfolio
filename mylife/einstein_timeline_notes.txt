Einstein Life Timeline - Key Locations and Dates
Source: https://einstein-website.de/en/timeline/

1879 - Born in Ulm, Germany (March 14)
1880 - Munich, Germany (family moves)
1881-1894 - Munich, Germany (childhood, school)
1894 - Milan/Pavia, Italy (family moves, Einstein joins them in December)
1895 - Aarau, Switzerland (school)
1896-1900 - Zurich, Switzerland (ETH Polytechnic student)
1901 - Winterthur, Switzerland (May-July teacher); Schaffhausen, Switzerland (Sept onwards)
1902-1909 - Bern, Switzerland (Patent Office, married Mileva, sons Hans Albert born 1904, miracle year 1905)
1909-1911 - Zurich, Switzerland (University professor, son Eduard born 1910)
1911-1912 - Prague, Bohemia/Czech (German University professor)
1912-1914 - Zurich, Switzerland (ETH professor)
1914-1932 - Berlin, Germany (Prussian Academy, divorced Mileva 1919, married Elsa, General Relativity 1916, Nobel Prize 1920)
1915 - Finishes general theory of relativity
1916 - Publishes general theory of relativity
1917 - Takes over Kaiser Wilhelm Institute for Physics
1918 - German surrender in WWI
1919 - Divorced from Mileva, marries Elsa Löwenthal, solar eclipse confirms relativity
1920 - Nobel Prize for Physics (awarded later)
