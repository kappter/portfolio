Isaac Newton Life Timeline - Key Locations and Dates
Source: https://www.britannica.com/summary/Isaac-Newton-Timeline

1642 (Dec 25, 1642 Old Style / Jan 4, 1643 New Style) - Born in Woolsthorpe, Lincolnshire, England
1653 - Woolsthorpe (reunited with mother after stepfather's death)
1653-1661 - Grantham, England (grammar school)
1661-1696 - Cambridge, England (Trinity College - student, fellow, professor)
  1661 - Begins undergraduate studies
  1665 - Receives bachelor's degree
  1665-1667 - Plague years, returns to Woolsthorpe
  1667 - Elected fellow at Trinity College
  1668 - Invents reflecting telescope
  1669-1671 - Professor of mathematics at Trinity
  1672 - Sends paper on light to Royal Society
  1684-1687 - Writes and publishes Principia Mathematica
1696-1727 - London, England (Warden/Master of the Mint, Royal Society President)
  1696 - Appointed warden of the mint, moves to London
  1701 - Resigns from Cambridge
  1703 - Elected president of Royal Society
  1704 - Publishes Opticks
  1705 - Knighted by Queen Anne
  1727 - Dies in London (March 20/31), buried at Westminster Abbey
