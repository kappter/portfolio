Einstein Life Timeline - Complete Locations and Dates
Source: https://einstein-website.de/en/timeline/

1879 - Ulm, Germany (born March 14)
1880-1894 - Munich, Germany
1894 - Milan/Pavia, Italy (family moves, Einstein joins December)
1895 - Aarau, Switzerland
1896-1900 - Zurich, Switzerland (ETH student)
1901 - Winterthur & Schaffhausen, Switzerland
1902-1909 - Bern, Switzerland (Patent Office, miracle year 1905)
1909-1911 - Zurich, Switzerland (University professor)
1911-1912 - Prague, Bohemia/Czech
1912-1914 - Zurich, Switzerland (ETH)
1914-1932 - Berlin, Germany (Prussian Academy)
1921 - First USA visit (Princeton lectures)
1922 - Japan journey, Nobel Prize awarded
1923 - Palestine, Spain
1925 - South America (Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay)
1930-1931 - USA visits (Pasadena, CalTech)
1932 - Princeton, NJ (Institute for Advanced Study begins)
1933 - Emigrates to USA permanently (Princeton, NJ) - never returns to Germany
1935-1955 - Princeton, New Jersey (Mercer Street 112)
1955 - Dies in Princeton on April 18 (age 76)
