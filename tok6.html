TOK Common Topics Reference

A clickable classroom tool for discussing commonly used TOK Exhibition objects and Essay examples — not as a ban list, but as a way to move students from obvious examples toward meaningful knowledge encounters.

Originality in TOK usually comes less from the weirdness of the topic and more from the quality of the knowledge question.

A familiar object can be powerful when it is grounded in a real encounter with knowledge. A strange object can still be weak if the analysis is generic.

Commonly successful Accessible, rich, and easy to connect to TOK concepts.
Overused Still usable, but students must avoid stock analysis.
Original Best when connected to lived experience, not novelty alone.
Designed as a teacher-facing TOK planning and classroom discussion reference.
